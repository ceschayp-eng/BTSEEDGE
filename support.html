Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Your clients, engagements, time entries, invoices, expenses, settings and attached photographs are stored in BTSEEDGE's local app container on your iPhone. The app does not upload them or make network requests.

Can you recover or reset my records?

No. There is no BTSEEDGE account or cloud copy for us to access. Keep your own exports if you need an additional copy. If you still have access to the app, do not clear your workspace or delete the app before exporting what you need.

How do I export my records?

Open Studio, go to Your data, and prepare the CSV export you need. Then use the share button to choose where to save or send it. An export leaves the app only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual records or open Studio → Your data and choose Clear all records. Deleting BTSEEDGE also removes its local app data and attached photographs. These actions cannot be undone by support.

Why can't I take or attach a photo?

Check camera and photo permissions under your iPhone's Settings → BTSEEDGE. You can grant access only when you want to attach an invoice, receipt or other image. Other app features do not require these permissions.

Does BTSEEDGE send invoices or connect to my bank?

No. BTSEEDGE is a record-keeping tool. It cannot send invoices, take or move payments, file taxes, or connect to a bank, card or accounting service.

Are runway and tax reserve figures advice?

No. Calculations and projections use only the figures and assumptions you enter. They are not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice. Consult a qualified professional for advice about your circumstances.

Report a problem

When contacting us about a bug, include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The BTSEEDGE version shown in the app.
  • The steps that cause the issue.
  • What happened and what you expected instead.

A screenshot can help, but please hide client names, amounts and other private information before sending it.
