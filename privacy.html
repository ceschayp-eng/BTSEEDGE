Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BTSEEDGE handles information when you use the app and this website.

Effective date: November 2, 2026

On this page SummaryInformation you enterDevice permissionsSharing and exportsSupport communicationsRetention and deletionSecurityChildrenThis websiteChangesContact

1. Summary

BTSEEDGE does not collect your studio records. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no network requests. Information you enter is stored locally in the app's container on your iPhone.

We do not operate a server that receives your clients, engagements, time entries, invoices, expenses, settings or attached photographs. We do not sell or rent personal information.

2. Information you enter

You can enter information about your business and clients, including names, contact details, email addresses, rates, notes, engagement details, hours, invoice amounts, payment status, expenses and settings. You can also attach photographs to invoice and expense records.

This information is written to and read from BTSEEDGE's local app container on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Please avoid entering sensitive information that you do not need for your records.

3. Device permissions

Camera

If you choose to photograph an invoice, receipt or document, BTSEEDGE asks for camera access. The camera is used only when you initiate this feature.

Photo library

If you choose an existing image to attach to a record, BTSEEDGE asks for photo library access. Images you select are resized and saved as attachments in the app's local container.

You can deny either permission. The rest of the app remains available. You can change permissions later in your iPhone's Settings.

4. Sharing and exports

BTSEEDGE can prepare CSV files containing records you choose to export. A file remains on your device until you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. If you send or save an export using another app or service, that recipient's own privacy terms apply.

We do not receive a copy of your exports, and the app does not automatically share your information with third parties.

5. Support communications

If you email us at shuummaa09@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to your request, provide support, and maintain the security and reliability of the app.

Please do not email client records, financial account details, passwords or other sensitive information. Your email provider and ours process support messages under their own privacy policies. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for the request and related record-keeping.

6. Retention and deletion

Your app records remain on your device until you delete individual records, use the app's clear-all-records control, or delete BTSEEDGE. Deleting the app removes its local container, including records and attached photographs. This cannot be undone by us.

Because we do not hold your app data, we cannot access, restore or delete it remotely. Export a copy before clearing records or removing the app if you want to keep one. Copies you export are subject to your own storage and deletion choices.

7. Security

Local storage reduces exposure to remote services, but no method of storage is completely secure. Protect your iPhone with a passcode and keep its operating system up to date. You are responsible for protecting exported files and for maintaining any backups you want to keep.

8. Children's privacy

BTSEEDGE is a business record-keeping tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since app records stay on the user's device, parents or guardians should manage device access and app use as appropriate.

9. This website

This website contains no accounts, forms, advertising, analytics or cookies set by us. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and server logs to deliver and protect the site. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or its App Store listing where appropriate.
